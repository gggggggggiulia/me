01.

SUBSTRATA ↗

Music festival visual identity

Media Design university course
held by Davide Di Gennaro and Francesco Mantovani
2024

Printed poster serie
Digital animated poster serie
Folded map

SUBSTRATA is a unique festival of experimental electronic music: a concert that takes place quietly every day, under the green surface of leaves and in the intricate labyrinths of roots. plants and fungi orchestrate this concert in their own particular language, which is then translated into sound.

02.

Theremin 900 ↗

Gesture to sound interface

Touchdesigner workshop
held by Laura Arcangeli
2024

Touchdesigner interface

Born from an intensive five-day workshop in TouchDesigner by Laura Arcangeli of Studio Ultravioletto, this project explores the boundaries between the digital and the physical world. Merging the otherworldly Theremin with precise gesture-tracking courtesy of Leap Motion, the installation turns simple movements into immersive, multisensory experience.

03.

mappe invisibili

Soundmapping experience

BA Graduation Thesis in Design
2023

Mobile app prototype
Soundmap kit (map + postcards + pen)

mappe invisibili is an experience that takes place in the Natural Park of Monte San Bartolo. Through the practice of sound maps, visitors are offered a new way to explore their surroundings. The visitor is guided through a process of learning sound mapping, at the end of which they will create a personal sound map and upload it to the dedicated platform.

This project won second place prize in the Interaction & Experience Unirsm Design Contest 2024, held by Unirsm Design.

04.

Autopoiesi ↗

Network diagram for biological interactions exploration

Information design course
held by Giorgio Uboldi
2025

Desktop website

Autopoiesi aims to easily visualize interactions between species in a specific environment. By exploring the complex network of non-human interactions, one can better understand the importance of each individual species to the maintenance of the entire ecosystem.

05.

Unlocking the Grid ↗

Data Storytelling Longscroll for Siemens Energy

Accurat Studio

Paid Post for New York Times

Unlocking the Grid is a geography-driven storytelling experience that takes users on a journey through the history of the United States’ electric power system. Data becomes space. Geography becomes story.
